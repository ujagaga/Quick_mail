How It Works

Sign in with Google to get your device token.
To send an e-mail, you simply make an HTTP GET or HTTP POST request to
"{{ request.url.replace("/cgi-bin/cgi_serve.py", "") }}send" with parameters, all required:

  • token=<Your user token>
  • msg=<Html safe text message to send>
  • to=<Recipient e-mail address>
  • sub=<E-mail subject>

To send to multiple addresses, you may use a comma separated list of e-mails for the "to" parameter.

To prevent spam, you must wait at least {{ min_wait_time }} seconds between emails.
